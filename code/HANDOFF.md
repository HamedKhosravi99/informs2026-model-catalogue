## 2. Non-negotiables

- **Causality.** No outage-derived value from a timestamp after the prediction origin.
  Code review is part of judging; violation = disqualification. The permissive reading
  (training counties' values at timestamps ≤ t) was adopted by the user and tested — it
  gave no gain, so the shipped model uses only pre-h72 outage inputs and is compliant
  under either reading.
- **Never use** `severity_tier` (CV stratification only), `peak_pct`, `peak_customers`,
  `time_to_restore_h`, `split` as features.
- **NDA:** `DM_Train.csv`, `DM_Test.csv`, `sample_submission.csv` and all PDFs are
  git-ignored. The repo must stay private.
- **External data.** The rules require "self-contained code that reproduces the
  submitted predictions when run end-to-end on the provided data files" and are
  silent on external covariates. We are compliant under the plain reading —
  `data_static/` is committed, nothing is fetched at run time. Under the strict
  reading only the `f2` member is implicated (external statics + Open-Meteo
  sub-county weather; no other member uses outside data). The contingency is
  measured, not hypothetical: swapping `f2` for `C1` (same pipeline, provided
  data only) costs **+0.00004** on the blend, 0.00854 -> 0.00858. REPORT §5aq.
- **Do not exploit** the `N_t(71)`/`R_t(71)` artifact (they are centered 3-hour means, so
  they encode hour 72). Deliberate inversion would be computing an outage quantity for a
  future timestamp. Decision recorded in `REPORT.md` §5h.
  **Update 2026-09-02:** the revised `osi_methodology.pdf` states these values are
  "organizer-supplied features, valid at every observed hour including March 13 23:00, and
  do not constitute a temporal-causality violation." Using them as provided (which we do:
  hour 71 is kept, hour 72+ masked) is sanctioned. Inverting them to recover hour 72 is not.
- **Metric: CONFIRMED by `evaluation_procedure.pdf` (released 2026-09-02).** Per-horizon RMSE
  pooled over county-hours (our scorer reproduces it to machine precision; N_k 9009/8694/7560/
  6048 match), ranking by average rank across horizons, ties on t01h. MAE is not scored.
  Original reasoning, kept for the record:
- **Metric: interpreted from the guidelines and committed (2026-08-13), not asked.**
  The 2026 wording ("assessed across all four horizons") mirrors the 2025 instrument
  verbatim ("average rank across the two horizons using RMSE"), and MAE appears in no
  document of either year. Working assumption: **per-horizon RMSE, average rank across

## 3. Validation protocol (never change it)

5-fold county-holdout, stratified by state × severity tier, seed 42. Held-out counties
have every outage column NaN'd after hour 71 **before** feature construction, so the
same code path consumes the real test file. Scoring maps the trajectory into the four
submission columns exactly as the organizers will.

**Adoption requires** a plausible mechanism plus: paired county bootstrap
(`compare_models.py`), per-horizon and per-fold breakdown, hour-band and tier-4
performance, and error correlation with current members (`evaluate.py <cand> <ctrl>`).
Fold spread is ±0.003, so pooled differences under ~0.0005 are noise.

**Anything learned between folds must be nested.** FOUR techniques measured as wins and
reversed under honest nesting (the fourth added 2026-08-16: exhaustive member-subset
selection over 968 subsets, 0.008423 naive -> 0.008569 nested, worse than a-priori
equal weights; REPORT §5ax): greedy ensemble-weight selection (0.00883 → 0.00959),
big-pool NNLS over 63 models (0.00850 → 0.00959), ensemble distillation
(0.00892 → 0.00931).

## 4. Standing empirical laws (~100 experiments)

1. **Raising independent, deployment-relevant effective sample size wins.** Storm jitter,
   mixup and multi-origin all beat their un-augmented control. But origins 4 h apart are
   redundant (5 and 8 origins both lose to 3), and pre-event origins train on a regime
   that never occurs.
2. **Variance reduction wins.** Bagging beat boosting; 8 seeds beat 3; ensembling beat
   every single model.
3. **Adding inputs to sequence models loses** — three independent confirmations
   (sub-county channels, external statics, statics + scoring loss). The same information
   *helps trees*. At 191 training sequences each input dimension costs more variance than
   it returns.
4. **Specialisation loses** (per-state, per-horizon, severity-gated experts) because it
   fragments sample size.
5. **Fitting the combiner overfits**, monotonically with parameter count.
6. **Multiplicative corrections are dangerous** — county calibration, county-normalised
   targets and binned-classification reconstruction were all *worse than predicting
   zeros*; the R6 flow correction also failed.
7. **The √-scale objective helps exactly when the network predicts the scored quantity
   directly** (Transformer ✓, bi-encoder ✓, component model ✗ — its OSI is composed from
   raw-scale-supervised heads).
8. **RESTATED 2026-08-14 — the wording was far broader than the measurement.**
   The old claim ("county magnitude is not identifiable from the prefix, R² ≤ 0.08
   at any prefix length") came from one narrow construction in
   `identifiability_curves.py`: an *in-sample squared Pearson* of log prefix mass
   against the mass of a **fixed** sub-window (h121–215), no covariates, no
   holdout. Re-derived four ways on the deployment window:
   (a) that construction, h121–215: R² 0.014→0.078 as L grows — reproduces ≤0.08;
   (b) same construction, **deployment window h73–215**, L=72: **R² 0.372**;
   (c) out-of-fold county-holdout ridge/RF on the full legal feature set:
       **R² 0.55–0.60**; (d) the **shipped model's own** predicted-vs-true county
   mass: **R² 0.69 (log), 0.88 (raw)**.
   County magnitude is therefore *substantially* predictable — the models do it
   well. What is NOT recoverable is the **residual** multiplier after the model
   has spoken: regressing log(true mass / predicted mass) on every legal feature
   gives OOF R² ≈ 0 (Ridge +0.18 on the active half, RF negative), which is the
   quantity that actually blocks per-county correction. State the law that way.
   The six failed attacks (residual calibration, severity gating, restoration
   rates, retrieval, external infrastructure data, latent adaptation) all target
   the *residual*, so their verdicts stand unchanged.
9. **CORRECTED 2026-08-14 — timing is NOT free.** The old claim ("2% of MSE") came
   from a `np.roll` circular shift in `identifiability_curves.py`, which wrapped the
   quiet tail into the wave-1 decay head and penalised every nonzero shift. With an
   edge-padded physical shift the best per-county ±12 h offset removes **16.8% of
   MSE** (honest split-half: 15.5%), and only **19%** of counties want zero shift
   (not 61%). Consequences: E3's rejection (0.00912 vs 0.00897) was inside the real
   noise bar, not a clear reject — phase modelling is REOPENED; and the NOVELTY.md
   claim that "phase is free under known forcing" must be withdrawn from the report.
10. **The ensemble is saturated.** E2 is better than the model it supersedes *and* the
    most decorrelated candidate (0.848) — and adds nothing.
11. **P1's recursion buys decorrelation, not accuracy.** The non-recursive R8b ties it
    standalone (and halves its MAE) yet loses the ensemble seat: closed-loop rollout
    errs in a different way (correlation 0.752 vs 0.808), and that error basis is what
    the seat pays for. Stock-proportional restore is the load-bearing part — gross-flow
    restore without the stock level is unidentifiable and scores worse than zeros.
12. **Ensemble headroom is governed by error-basis diversity, not member quality —
    and diversity cannot be bought, only built.** A3 is better than `s62` at all four
    horizons (P 0.949) and correlates 0.980 — worth −0.00003. E2 was better and
    0.848-correlated — worth nothing. P1b is *worse* standalone (0.01042) and
    0.752-correlated — it earned the seat. Sweep 14 added the mechanism boundary:
    training FOR decorrelation (NCL within-group; anti-correlation vs the shipped
    ensemble's error vector) hits a **floor at corr ≈ 0.80** that no penalty strength
    breaks, and pays for every point of diversity at par in accuracy (N2: corr 0.801
    at solo 0.01209 — refused by the seat test). Only a structurally different
    computation has ever measured below the floor, and its decorrelation is free.
    Rank candidates by error correlation first, standalone score second — and get
    the correlation from structure, not from the loss.
13c. **Variance reduction buys solo accuracy by SPENDING error diversity, and
    the exchange rate depends on whether the blend partner shares the technique
    (REPORT §5at).** Completing the seed x snapshot grid: i11's best solo variant
    is 25 seeds x 4 snapshots (0.00892) but it makes the blend significantly
    WORSE (0.008568 vs 0.008532, P 0.011, CI excludes zero). Correlation with
    a3x25 rises monotonically with every variance-reduction step (0.864 -> 0.916
    -> 0.936 -> 0.950), and snapshots raise it faster than seeds *at worse solo
    quality* — because a3x25 is itself a snapshot ensemble. Do not apply the
    partner's own technique to the other members.
13b. **Seed scaling pays in proportion to a member's UN-AVERAGED variance
    (REPORT §5am).** 25 seeds help i11 (−0.00057), s35 (−0.00032) and s62
    (−0.00035) — all single-model trainers — and HURT s39 (+0.00019) and s40
    (+0.00023), which already average internally (attention pool, three
    origins). Upgrading i11+s35 improves the 8-member ensemble at 4/4 horizons
    (0.008621 → 0.008583) and the shipped blend to **0.008534**. Folded into
    the W17 freeze rebuild; do NOT seed-scale s39/s40.
13. **Schedule diversity beats seed diversity — and not just on cost.** At equal
    compute: snapshots 0.00869/303 s vs 8 seeds 0.00883/346 s. At matched 25 seeds
    the gap is *significant* (A3x25 0.00862 vs A1x25 0.00894, P 0.023, CI excludes
    zero): plain seed averaging converges to a worse limit (~0.0089) that no seed
    count escapes, while cyclic restarts visit minima independent early-stopped
    seeds never reach. Best single model on record: A3x25, 0.00862/0.00247 — ties
    the shipped ensemble (P 0.630); not adopted (fourth-decimal difference).
14. **The metric can be chased along the hour axis and not along the county axis.**
    S44's hour reweighting works (it reallocates within every sequence); county
    reweighting degrades monotonically (γ = 0/0.25/0.50 → 0.00897/0.00902/0.00918)
    because it reduces effective sample size, and this project is sample-size-bound.
15. **P1's rollout drift was an estimator artifact — E[h(X)] vs h(E[X]) — not a
    modeling failure.** Monte-Carlo rollout (REPORT §5u, `src/ideas9.py` M1c:
    Bernoulli damage occurrence, mean-exact residual ratios, restore kept
    deterministic) ties p1b on RMSE with **no DAgger stage at all** and cuts its
    MAE 0.00447 → 0.00300. The deterministic rollout's `hrs_since_damage` is
    pegged at exactly 0 for 100% of steps (training truth: mean 7.2 h) — DAgger
    was compensating a self-inflicted state mismatch that the correct estimator
    simply doesn't have. Do NOT sample the restore fraction: the clip at 1
    truncates the pool's heavy tail (~7–10%/step under-restoration, audit-found).
    Seat decision unchanged (m1c corr 0.751 ≈ p1b's 0.752; swap +0.00003, P
    0.267) — fifth saturation confirmation. **The MAE contingency is now the m1c
    swap (0.00867/0.00261), superseding the r8b swap (0.00873/0.00260).**

## 7. Gotchas that already cost time

- **Open-Meteo stamps in EDT, the competition uses fixed EST.** A one-hour offset made
  sub-county weather look useless (0.00963) until realigned (0.00942). Always check lag
  correlation against the provided `gust` before concluding a feature is worthless.
- **Verify background jobs by reading their output**, not their exit status. A crashed
  `make_submission.py` once left a stale submission on disk that looked fine.
- **Editing `ENSEMBLE_FINAL_W` by slicing** once deleted the adjacent `TAIL_SPLIT_HOUR` /
  `TAIL_SCALE` constants. Import the module after editing it.
- **`N_t`/`R_t` are centered 3-hour rolling means** of the gross flows, not per-hour
  deltas; `D_t` is the trailing 6-hour mean of `P_t`. OSI is reconstructible from stock +
  gross flows to RMSE 3.4e-05.
- **Zero-initialising both a latent and its projection** is a saddle — neither learns.
- **XGBoost/LightGBM need libomp**, which is not installed system-wide. It was fetched
  from conda-forge into `the job's lib directory` and the wheels' rpath patched with
  `install_name_tool`. If they stop importing, redo that.
